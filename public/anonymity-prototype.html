Anonymity · Final step

Verify the employee. Unlink the question.

The recommended architecture uses a one-time blind privacy pass so eligibility verification and question submission are handled separately.

1Google SSOConfirms the person is an approved employee.
2Blind passThe verifier signs a hidden one-time token.
3UnlinkThe browser reveals a valid token the issuer never saw.
4Submit separatelyThe question is sent without the employee login session.
5Minimise cluesMinimal logs and delayed or batched writes reduce correlation.

What this improves

HR and ordinary database readers cannot identify an author. Even a platform administrator cannot directly connect the login identity to the submitted token.

What we must still explain

Small groups, writing style, details inside the message and sophisticated traffic analysis can still create inference. Strong anonymity is not an absolute guarantee.
